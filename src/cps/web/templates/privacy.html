Privacy

This page says what this service keeps about you, why, where and for how long.

What is kept

  • Your timetable: its link, if you gave one, and its content as last read.
  • What you typed: exams, deadlines, busy times, how many hours you study, days off.
  • Your plan, and which sessions you reported as done, skipped or hard.
  • A log of what happened to your plan (created, settings changed, a session reported, with the date), to count how often plans are used. It holds no timetable content.

Not asked for and not kept: your name, your e-mail address, a password. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The service's own log records the time, the kind of page and the answer's status, not your plan's address. The company that hosts the server may log connections, with IP addresses, under its own policy.

Why

To make your study plan and keep it up to date, which is what you ask for by using the service. Nothing is sold, shared or used for anything else.

Where, and who can see it

On the server that runs this service. Your plan's two addresses (the page and the calendar feed) are the only keys to it: anyone who has them can see your plan, and the page's address lets them change it. Keep them private, as you would a calendar's secret address. Your calendar app reads the feed; Google Calendar, for one, reads it from Google's servers.

For how long

Until {{ retention }} days after your last exam or deadline, then deleted automatically. You can delete everything at once from Settings, "Delete my plan and all my data". Backups of the server's data are kept for at most 7 days.

Your rights
