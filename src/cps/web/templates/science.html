{{ _("The research: in 317 experiments, the gap between reviews that gave the best memory grew with how long the memory had to last (Cepeda and colleagues, 2006). In a study of more than 1,350 people, the best gap was about 20 to 40 % of the time to a test a week away, and 5 to 10 % of a year away (Cepeda and colleagues, 2008).") }}

{{ _("The maths") }}

S′ = S · (1 + a · (11 − D) · S−b · (ec(1 − R) − 1))

{{ _h("After a successful recall the stability S grows. The gain is larger for an easier topic (lower difficulty D), smaller for one already solid (the S−b), and larger the lower your recall R was when you made it (the last term, which is zero at R = 100 %): that is the spacing effect, written as a formula.") }}
