What it does, and what it does not

  • It meets deadlines first, earliest due first, and says early when one will not fit.
  • Before each exam it plans timed practice; between lectures it plans short self-tests on what is fading, because testing yourself beats rereading.
  • It keeps to your weekly hours and days off, and leaves the rest of your time free.
  • It does not know how well you know a subject until you tell it: after each session, one tap says done, skipped or hard, and the plan changes.
